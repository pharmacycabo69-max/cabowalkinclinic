Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Emergency Care in Cabo

What counts as a real emergency I should call you for?

Anything that worries you and is more than a minor scrape. Chest pain, severe shortness of breath, suspected stroke, fainting, severe abdominal pain, head injury, major bleeding, severe allergic reaction, broken bones, deep cuts, severe dehydration, high fever in a child — and many less dramatic situations where you just want a doctor’s eyes on a problem. Call us first; we triage in minutes.

Should I just go to the hospital myself?

For an immediate life-threatening emergency call 911 (Mexico’s emergency number) at the same time as calling us. For everything else, calling us first is usually faster and clearer: we either treat you at your hotel or our clinic, or we transport you to the right hospital with English-speaking coordination, an itemized invoice, and a bilingual advocate by your side.

Which hospital do you escalate to?

Our partner hospital is a leading private hospital in Los Cabos with internationally certified specialists, 24/7 emergency, ICU, operating rooms and advanced imaging — and a clinical team that also speaks English. Medical decisions are clinical: if a different facility is more appropriate for your condition, we’ll tell you and arrange it.

How fast can a doctor get to my hotel?

Most house calls in Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo and the Tourist Corridor arrive within 45 minutes. If we determine you need a hospital instead, we dispatch ground ambulance directly.

What does an urgent care visit cost?

Walk-in clinic visit from $80 USD. Video visit with our English-speaking doctor $59 USD. Hotel or villa house call $200 USD. Hospital-level care is billed by the hospital separately. We quote in USD up front and provide a fully itemized invoice in English for your travel insurance.

Do you take insurance?

At our clinic we don’t bill insurance directly — most travelers pay at the time of service and submit our itemized English invoice (with codes) for reimbursement, which is faster and avoids surprises. For hospital-level care our partner hospital works with a wide network of national and international insurers, and we help you verify your specific coverage before you commit.
