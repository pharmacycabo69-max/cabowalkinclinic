What we treat at clinic level

  • Stomach issues: traveler's diarrhea, food poisoning, vomiting, abdominal pain.
  • Dehydration and heat illness: IV fluids and electrolyte therapy.
  • Infections: sinus, ear, throat, urinary and skin.
  • Cuts, sprains and minor injuries: sutures, splints, dressings.
  • Fevers, flu and COVID: testing and treatment, including pediatric care.
  • Allergic reactions, rashes, jellyfish and stingray stings.
  • Migraines, anxiety and panic attacks: acute treatment in a calm setting.
  • Hangover recovery and severe dehydration: IV therapy at your hotel.

When we escalate to a partner hospital

Some situations need more than a clinic can give. We stay with you as your bilingual advocate.

Trauma & serious injury

Bad falls, road accidents, deep wounds, head injuries that may need imaging, surgery or admission.

Cardiac, stroke, respiratory

Chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe shortness of breath, anaphylaxis.

Surgical emergencies

Appendicitis, gallbladder, bowel obstruction, severe infections.

Obstetric & pediatric urgent

Pregnancy complications and serious pediatric illness.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a real emergency I should call you for?

Anything that worries you and is more than a minor scrape. Chest pain, severe shortness of breath, suspected stroke, fainting, severe abdominal pain, head injury, major bleeding, severe allergic reaction, broken bones, deep cuts, severe dehydration, high fever in a child — and many less dramatic situations where you just want a doctor’s eyes on a problem. Call us first; we triage as quickly as we can.

Should I just go to the hospital myself?

For an immediate life-threatening emergency call 911 (Mexico’s emergency number) at the same time as calling us. For everything else, calling us first is usually faster and clearer: we either treat you at your hotel or our clinic, or we transport you to the right hospital with English-speaking coordination, an itemized invoice, and a bilingual advocate by your side.

Which hospital do you escalate to?

Our partner hospital is a leading private hospital in Los Cabos with internationally certified specialists, 24/7 emergency, ICU, operating rooms and advanced imaging — and a clinical team that also speaks English. Medical decisions are clinical: if a different facility is more appropriate for your condition, we’ll tell you and arrange it.

How fast can a doctor get to my hotel?

Most house calls in Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo and the Tourist Corridor arrive at a time we confirm with you when you book. If we determine you need a hospital instead, we dispatch ground ambulance directly.

What does an urgent care visit cost?

Walk-in clinic visit $99 USD. Video visit with our English-speaking doctor $59 USD. Hotel or villa house call $189 USD. Hospital-level care is billed by the hospital separately. We quote in USD up front and provide a fully itemized invoice in English for your travel insurance.

Do you take insurance?

At our clinic we don’t bill insurance directly — most travelers pay at the time of service and submit our itemized English invoice (with codes) for reimbursement, which is faster and avoids surprises. For hospital-level care our partner hospital works with a wide network of national and international insurers, and we help you verify your specific coverage before you commit.
