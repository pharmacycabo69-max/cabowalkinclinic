The Most Common Reasons Travelers Need Imaging in Cabo

  • Possible fractures from falls, ATV/UTV accidents, scooter spills, slips on wet pool decks, or sports injuries.
  • Head injuries — any loss of consciousness, persistent headache, vomiting or confusion after a hit. CT to rule out bleeding.
  • Severe abdominal pain — possible appendicitis, gallstones, kidney stones, diverticulitis.
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath — chest X-ray and CT as needed to rule out serious causes.
  • Back and neck injuries — pool diving, snorkeling/surfing wipeouts, or sleeping wrong in a strange bed.
  • Suspected DVT after a long flight or sedentary period.
  • Obstetric concerns for pregnant travelers — ultrasound for reassurance or to assess a concern.

Insurance, Pricing & What to Expect

Imaging fees are billed by the hospital, not by our clinic, and vary by the study (X-ray is the most affordable; MRI is the most expensive). We help you get a quote in advance whenever it is not an immediate emergency. For travelers with international travel insurance (Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global and others), our partner hospital works with a wide network of insurers; we will help you verify your specific coverage before you commit to a non-urgent study. You will receive an itemized English invoice that supports a reimbursement claim if you pay out of pocket. We promise the process — clear quotes, English documentation, help with the paperwork — not a specific reimbursement outcome.

FAQ

Common Questions About Imaging in Cabo

Can I get an X-ray today in Cabo San Lucas?

In most urgent cases, yes. After our doctor sees you and confirms an X-ray is indicated, we fast-track a same-day appointment at the partner hospital. Walk-in self-scheduling at a hospital imaging desk in a foreign country is usually slower and confusing — calling us first is faster.

Where exactly is the imaging performed?

Imaging is performed at a leading private hospital in Los Cabos with on-site radiology — digital X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound — read by qualified radiologists. We arrange the appointment, the transport if needed, and the follow-up. Our doctor reviews the results with you in English.

How much will it cost?

Imaging fees are set and billed by the hospital. X-ray is the most affordable; MRI is the most expensive; CT and ultrasound fall in between. For non-emergency studies we help you get a quote in advance. Our clinic’s coordination fee (the doctor’s visit that orders and interprets the imaging) is separate and quoted up front in USD.

Will my travel insurance pay for the imaging?

Most international travel-insurance policies cover medically necessary imaging. You will receive a fully itemized invoice in English for reimbursement. For larger studies (CT, MRI) we can help you verify coverage with our partner hospital before you commit. We coordinate the paperwork; the final reimbursement decision is made by your insurer.

Can I bring my imaging files home?

Yes — you’ll be given the imaging on a CD/USB and/or via electronic transfer, plus the radiologist’s report. We’re happy to send these to your physician at home in English on request, with your written permission.

What if the imaging shows something serious?

If the study shows something that needs hospital admission, surgery, or a specialist — we coordinate it. The same partner hospital has 24/7 emergency care, an ICU, operating rooms and a deep specialist roster. If you would prefer to fly home for treatment, we coordinate ambulance and air ambulance, including medical repatriation.
