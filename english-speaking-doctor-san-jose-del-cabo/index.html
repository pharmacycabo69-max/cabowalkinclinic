Common reasons travelers call us in San José

Because San José hosts so many families and longer-stay visitors, we frequently treat children’s fevers and ear infections, stomach bugs, post-flight dehydration and jet lag, UTIs, and chronic-condition questions away from home. Just landed at SJD feeling unwell? A doctor or an IV recovery drip can be at your hotel the same day.

Prescriptions, labs and insurance

Our doctor can prescribe medication and arrange to your San José hotel, plus mobile lab tests if needed. Every visit comes with an itemized English invoice for travel-insurance reimbursement.

Family-friendly care, close to the airport

San José del Cabo draws families, couples and longer-stay visitors, and our care reflects that. We see a lot of children — fevers, ear infections, rashes and the stomach bugs that travel so easily — and a parent is always present and involved in every decision. We also care for older travelers managing chronic conditions away from their usual doctor, and we’re happy to coordinate with the medications and instructions you brought from home. Because San José is the side of Los Cabos closest to the airport, timing matters on arrival and departure days: if you land feeling rough or wake up unwell before a flight, a same-day house call or a quick video visit can sort out a prescription, a fit-to-fly question or simple reassurance. Our physicians can order mobile lab tests when something needs investigating and arrange so you never have to track down a farmacia in an unfamiliar town. It’s the calm, English-speaking medical backup that lets families relax and enjoy the quieter side of Cabo.
