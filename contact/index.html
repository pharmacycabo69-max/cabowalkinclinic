Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome 9 AM to 5 PM every day. You can also book online or message us on WhatsApp before you come.

What is the best way to reach you?

WhatsApp or call +52 624 226 1023. A person replies in English or Spanish during clinic hours; after-hours visits are by appointment and confirmed by a doctor.

Do you accept travel insurance?

We accept US and Canada health and travel insurance subject to verification. We review your policy privately and confirm coverage and payment before a non-urgent visit. Self-pay is always available.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911. We are not an emergency room.
