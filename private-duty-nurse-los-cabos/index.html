Dedicated, one-on-one care

Unlike a scheduled visit, a private duty nurse is present and focused entirely on one patient — administering medication and injections, managing wounds and IVs, monitoring vitals, assisting with mobility and personal care, and watching continuously for changes. It is ideal for complex recovery, dementia care, or simply when a family wants a professional present so they can be a family rather than caregivers. A physician supervises and is available to step in.

Flexible coverage across Los Cabos

It starts with a free assessment to understand the situation and build a care plan. A bilingual registered nurse comes to you with everything needed, delivers the care, and documents it. When dedicated, continuous care is the right call

The defining feature of private duty nursing is undivided attention. A hospital nurse cares for many patients at once; a brief home visit covers specific tasks and leaves. A private duty nurse is present and focused on one person, which changes what is possible — continuous monitoring catches subtle changes early, care is delivered exactly when needed rather than on a rounds schedule, and the patient and family have a consistent, trusted professional they come to know. That continuity matters enormously for complex recovery, high-acuity needs, and dementia care where constant supervision keeps someone safe. It is equally about the family: having a dedicated Nurse present means relatives can step back from exhausting round-the-clock caregiving and simply be family again, confident their loved one is in capable hands. We match the right nurse to the situation, rotate professionals for 24/7 coverage with careful handoffs, and a physician oversees throughout. For situations that need more than scheduled visits, dedicated private duty nursing is the gold standard.
