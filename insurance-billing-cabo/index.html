What's on the Invoice You Submit to Your Insurer

A reimbursable invoice has to be specific. Yours will include:

  • Patient name, date of birth, and date(s) of service.
  • The treating provider's full name and Mexican medical license (cédula profesional).
  • The clinic's name, address and tax ID (RFC).
  • An itemized list of services with codes (ICD-10 diagnosis codes and CPT-style procedure codes where applicable).
  • Quantities, unit prices in USD, totals, and confirmation of payment received.
  • A brief clinical summary of the visit when relevant for the claim.

If your insurer needs anything else after you submit, contact us — we will provide the additional documentation in English at no charge.

Travel-Insurance Reimbursement — What to Expect

Most international travel-insurance policies — Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global, World Nomads, Manulife, Blue Cross Canada, and many others — reimburse medically necessary urgent care, ambulance and emergency hospitalization, subject to your policy’s terms. We are not your insurer; what we promise is the part we control: a clear, code-rich invoice and prompt help with follow-up questions. The actual reimbursement amount and timeline depend entirely on your policy, your deductible and your insurer’s review.

Practical tip: the moment you decide to call us, also call your travel insurer’s 24/7 emergency assistance line (the number on the back of your card or in your policy app). Many policies require notification within 24 hours of an emergency or hospitalization to preserve full benefits.

Medical Evacuation & Repatriation Coverage

Air ambulance and medical repatriation from Mexico to the U.S. or Canada typically run between US $25,000 and US $60,000 out of pocket. Many quality travel-insurance policies include emergency medical evacuation and repatriation benefits of $250,000 to $1,000,000 — pre-approval is almost always required. We help you contact your insurer’s assistance line, gather the medical documents they request, and arrange the receiving hospital so the evacuation can be authorized. FAQ

Common Insurance & Billing Questions

Do you accept my insurance?

At our clinic we don’t bill insurance directly — most travelers pay at the time of service and we provide an itemized invoice in English with the codes your insurer needs for reimbursement. For hospital-level care, our partner hospital works with a wide network of national and international insurers; we help you verify whether yours is included before any non-immediate step.

Which travel-insurance policies reimburse a visit here?

Most international travel-insurance and major-medical policies — Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global, World Nomads, Manulife, Blue Cross Canada and many others — reimburse medically necessary urgent care performed by a licensed provider abroad. We provide documentation; the reimbursement decision is your insurer’s.

What documentation do I need from you to file my claim?

A fully itemized invoice in English with diagnostic codes (ICD-10), procedure codes, provider name and professional ID (cédula), clinic name and tax info, dates of service, prices in USD and confirmation of payment. If your insurer needs anything else, we provide it in English at no charge.

Can you direct-bill any insurer?

At our clinic the standard model is pay-and-reimburse — direct billing involves carrier-specific contracts and approvals that are almost never faster for the traveler. For larger hospital-level care, our partner hospital handles direct billing with many insurers when pre-authorization is approved; we coordinate that process for you in English.

Does Medicare or my U.S. health insurance work in Mexico?

Traditional U.S. Medicare and most domestic U.S. health plans do not cover care outside the United States. Some Medicare Advantage plans offer limited emergency coverage abroad. Canadian provincial plans provide minimal coverage abroad. Travel insurance fills the gap.

What if I can't pay at the time of service?

Talk to us. We can sometimes work out an arrangement, especially when an insurance pre-authorization is in progress. We would rather solve the problem than turn a sick traveler away. The cleanest path is to use a major credit card and submit the itemized invoice for reimbursement.
