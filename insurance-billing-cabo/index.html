How your request can end

  • Pending review
  • More information needed

Every request is reviewed by a person on our team. Whatever the result, we tell you in writing what is covered and what you would pay, and you can always choose to be seen as a self-pay patient.

Which insurance we accept

We can review

  • Health insurance plans from the United States
  • Health insurance plans from Canada
  • Travel medical insurance from the US or Canada
  • Only when your policy covers the service in Mexico

We do not accept

  • Mexican national insurance plans (IMSS, ISSSTE or Mexican private insurers)
  • Coverage we have not verified before a non-urgent visit

Acceptance is case by case. We do not guarantee coverage, pre-authorization, reimbursement, direct payment by your insurer or the absence of a deductible. Your policy’s limits, exclusions and deductible apply.

How verification works

  1. Contact usSend a WhatsApp message with your name and how to reach you. No medical details or documents needed yet.
  2. Share your policy privatelyOur team tells you exactly which information is needed and how to share it securely. We only ask for what is strictly necessary.
  3. We review itA member of our team checks whether your policy can cover the service in Mexico and how payment would work.
  4. You get the result in writingCoverage confirmed, more information needed, or not confirmed — with the payment arrangement, any deductible and your responsibility spelled out before a non-urgent service.
  5. We see youWith your insurance as agreed, or as a self-pay patient if you prefer or if coverage is not confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept my insurance?

If it is a health or travel insurance plan from the United States or Canada that covers care in Mexico, we can review it. Message us to start the verification; we confirm in writing before a non-urgent service.

Do you accept Mexican insurance?

No. We only review international health and travel insurance from the United States and Canada.

Will you bill my insurer directly?

It depends on your policy. We confirm the payment arrangement with you case by case after verification. We do not promise direct billing in advance.

Will my US health insurance or Medicare cover care in Mexico?

Many US health plans, and Medicare in most cases, do not cover care outside the United States, while travel medical insurance often does. It depends on your policy: message us and we verify it before your visit.

What if my coverage is not confirmed?

You can still be seen as a self-pay patient at our published USD prices and receive an itemized invoice in English to submit to your insurer.

How do I share my policy safely?

Start with a WhatsApp message with only your name. Our team then tells you how to share the policy privately. Please don’t post documents in public forms or reviews.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911 first. Insurance checks never delay emergency guidance.
