Knowing the SJD airport area

SJD sits about 20–30 minutes from most San José and Corridor hotels, with the immediate airport area itself being mostly transit rather than lodging. That means our service really shines at your hotel on your first or last day, rather than at the terminal. If you land unwell, we meet you at your arrival hotel; if you’re flying out and need a check or a note, we come to wherever you’re staying and work within your departure timeline. For fit-to-fly requirements, plan a day or two of buffer so results and paperwork are ready.

Bookend your trip with care

The two highest-risk moments of any Cabo trip, health-wise, are the day you arrive and the day you leave. Arrival often means stepping off a long, dehydrating flight into intense heat — a post-flight drip at your first hotel turns a groggy first day into a strong start. Departure is the trickier one: if you or your child wakes up sick before a flight, you may need a doctor’s assessment, anti-nausea medication to travel comfortably, or a fit-to-fly note. Build in buffer for anything that requires a test result. Because we’re a mobile service anchored in San José del Cabo near SJD, we can reach your hotel quickly on either bookend, and we keep everything documented in English for your records and any insurance claim. If you’re flying out within hours and feeling unsure, start with a video visit — a bilingual doctor can quickly tell you whether you’re fine to fly or need to be seen first.

Frequently asked questions

I landed at SJD feeling sick — can you help at my hotel?

Yes. Post-flight illness and dehydration are very common. We can have a doctor or a post-flight IV drip at your San José or Corridor hotel the same day.

Can you do a fit-to-fly check before my flight from SJD?

Yes. A bilingual doctor can assess you and provide a fit-to-fly sign-off, including any required test. Plan a day or two of buffer so results and paperwork are ready in time.

Do you have a doctor at the airport terminal?

The airport area is mostly transit, so we provide care at your hotel rather than the terminal — typically 20–30 minutes from SJD, on your arrival or departure day.

Can you sort a prescription before I travel?

Yes. A doctor can prescribe what you need and we deliver it to your hotel, with an English invoice for insurance.
