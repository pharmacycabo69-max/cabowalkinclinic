Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome from 9 AM to 10 PM every day. You can also message us on WhatsApp first to check the wait.

How much is a walk-in visit?

Walk-in doctor visits start at $80 USD. Any extra services are explained before they are done.

Do you accept travel insurance?

We do not bill insurers directly, but we give you an itemized invoice in English that you can submit for reimbursement.

Can you see children?

Yes. Our doctors see adults and children for everyday issues during a trip.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911. We are not an emergency room.
