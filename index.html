Frequently asked questions

Answers for travelers in Cabo

How fast can a doctor or nurse get to my hotel?

Most house calls and IV visits arrive within 30–45 minutes anywhere in Cabo San Lucas, Médano, Pedregal, and the Marina. Visits to the Corridor and San José del Cabo are typically 45–75 minutes depending on traffic.

Are your doctors and nurses English-speaking?

Our doctors and nurses are English-speaking and experienced with international visitors. The ingredients used in our IV drips are medical-grade and sterile, and a short health screening is done before every service.

Do you accept travel insurance?

We don't bill travel insurance directly, but we issue a fully itemized invoice in English with all relevant codes that you can submit to Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global, or most major travel-insurance providers for reimbursement.

How much does an IV drip cost?

Our IV menu starts at $149 USD for a Hydration drip and goes up to $199 USD for our Recovery & Rejuvenation drip. NAD+ is $189. Doctor house calls start at $200 USD, and walk-in clinic visits at our Downtown location start at $80 USD.

Do you offer a group or travel-party discount?

Yes. If you book 3 or more IV sessions at once (for example, a bachelorette weekend, a corporate retreat, or a fishing trip) we apply an automatic group discount. Ask our medical concierge for the current rate when you call.

What if I'm in a private villa or on a yacht?

We cover private villas, gated communities (Pedregal, Querencia, Palmilla), and yachts in the Cabo San Lucas Marina. Just make sure security or the captain is expecting us so we can be cleared through.

Do I need an appointment?

For walk-in care at our Downtown Cabo San Lucas clinic, no — we are open 9 AM to 10 PM daily and welcome walk-ins. For house calls and IV therapy, just call or WhatsApp and we'll schedule the next available window, usually the same hour.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash (USD or MXN), Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. Payment is collected at the time of service. We do not require a deposit to dispatch.

Can you treat children?

Yes. We treat both adults and children, and our doctors regularly handle pediatric ear infections, stomach issues, fevers, and minor injuries on vacation.
