Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

Do you come to my hotel or all-inclusive resort?

Yes. We visit any hotel, all-inclusive resort, villa, Airbnb, condo or yacht in Los Cabos, from Médano Beach and Pedregal to the Tourist Corridor and San José del Cabo. Tell us your hotel and room number and we coordinate with the front desk.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome from 9 AM to 5 PM every day. You can also message us on WhatsApp before you come.

How much is a walk-in visit?

Walk-in doctor visits are $99 USD. Any extra services are explained before they are done.

Do you accept travel insurance?

Yes. We accept international health and travel insurance from the United States and Canada, subject to policy verification and coverage confirmation. Message us to verify your eligibility before your visit; self-pay is always available.

Can you see children?

Yes. Our doctors see adults and children for everyday issues during a trip.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911. We are not an emergency room.
