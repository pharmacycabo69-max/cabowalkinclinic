Medical support for your guests in Cabo San Lucas

One contact for your front desk, concierge or villa team: English-speaking licensed doctors, a walk-in clinic downtown, house calls to the property and coordination of lab tests and X-rays. Every request gets a folio and a clear status.

Who we work with

Hotels and resorts, concierges, property managers and villa administrators, travel agencies, companies and event organizers, tour and nautical operators.

Doctor at the property

A licensed doctor visits the guest’s room, villa or yacht in Cabo San Lucas and the Tourist Corridor. $189 USD per visit, tax included, paid by the guest.

Walk-in clinic downtown

Guests who can travel can walk in, 9 AM–5 PM daily, at Francisco I. Madero, Centro. Clinic visit $99 USD, tax included.

Tests and imaging, coordinated

When the doctor orders them, we coordinate lab tests and X-rays with licensed partners and share a quote first.

Paperwork for insurers

Itemized receipts in English and, when it applies, the clinical documents the guest needs to claim from their insurer. Coverage is never guaranteed.

How it works for your team

  1. Send the request. WhatsApp, phone or our booking page. Share only the guest’s name, contact number and location: health details go directly to the doctor.
  2. Get a folio. Every request has a folio, so your team can follow it without exchanging medical information.
  3. We confirm with the guest. We confirm availability and the time with the guest and tell you when it is confirmed, if the guest agrees.
  4. Care and receipt. The guest pays the clinic directly and receives an itemized receipt.

Emergencies: for chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding or loss of consciousness, call 911 and take the guest to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for a booking.

What we do and do not promise

  • Clear prices, in USD, tax included
  • A folio and a status for every request
  • Bilingual doctors with cédula profesional you can check
  • Guest privacy: we do not share health information with the property
  • No exclusivity agreements and no guaranteed arrival times
  • No commissions or referral fees are offered
  • We never use your brand or logo without written permission
  • Visits outside clinic hours depend on doctor availability

Questions from hotels and concierges

Do you pay commissions for referrals?

No. We do not offer commissions or referral fees. Guests pay the clinic directly at the published prices.

Which areas do you cover?

House calls in Cabo San Lucas and the Tourist Corridor. Other areas are confirmed case by case, depending on doctor availability.

Can the hotel be told about the guest’s condition?

Only with the guest’s consent. By default we only confirm that the visit took place.

Is this an emergency service?

No. For emergencies call 911 or go to the nearest hospital.
