For medical-tourism patients

Cabo and nearby cities draw visitors for cosmetic, dental and other procedures, and proper aftercare is critical to a good result. We coordinate with your surgeon’s instructions, provide the daily nursing your recovery needs, watch for complications, and escalate to a physician immediately if anything looks off. For any recovery

You do not have to be a medical tourist — anyone recovering from a procedure or illness while in Los Cabos can have nursing come to them, with single, daily or 24/7 private-duty visits arranged after a free assessment.

How in-home nursing works

It starts with a free assessment — by phone, video or in person — where we understand the situation and build a care plan. A bilingual registered nurse comes to you with everything needed, delivers the care, and documents it. We arrange a single visit or recurring visits (daily, several times a day, or 24/7) for the length of your stay, all overseen by a physician who can step in. You receive an itemized invoice for insurance.

Recovery is where outcomes are won or lost

Surgeons will tell you that a good surgical result depends heavily on the recovery, and the days immediately after a procedure are when complications either get caught early or quietly take hold. That is why dedicated post-op nursing matters so much. A trained nurse watching your incision daily spots the first signs of infection before it becomes serious; correct medication and injection administration (including post-op blood thinners) prevents problems; proper mobility support avoids setbacks; and a physician a call away means anything concerning is assessed immediately. For medical-tourism patients far from their surgeon, this professional safety net is invaluable — the alternative is recovering alone with worrying questions and no one to catch a developing problem. We coordinate with your surgeon’s instructions, provide the level of care your recovery needs from daily visits to 24/7, and document everything. Recovering well in a calm hotel or villa with expert nursing protects both your result and your peace of mind — which is exactly the point of investing in proper aftercare.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide post-op nursing for medical tourists in Cabo?

Yes. Bilingual nurses provide in-home post-operative care — wound and drain care, injections, mobility help and monitoring — coordinated with your surgeon and overseen by a physician.

What does post-op nursing include?

Incision and wound care, drain and device management, prescribed injections and medication, mobility help, vitals and monitoring, and IV treatment if ordered, with physician oversight.

Can you provide 24/7 post-op care?

Yes. After a free assessment we can arrange daily or round-the-clock private-duty nursing during your recovery.

What if a complication appears?

Our nurses watch for warning signs and escalate immediately to a supervising physician, who can examine you and coordinate care or referral.
