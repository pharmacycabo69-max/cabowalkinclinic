About us

English-speaking doctors for Los Cabos, at the clinic, at your hotel or by video

Cabo Walk-In Clinic is a downtown Cabo San Lucas clinic run by licensed Mexican doctors and nurses who speak English. We look after travelers and residents the same day, with clear USD prices and paperwork your insurer understands.

Why we started

Getting sick on vacation is stressful: a new country, a language barrier and no idea what a visit will cost. We built Cabo Walk-In Clinic to remove all three. You see a licensed doctor who explains everything in English, you know the price before we start, and you leave with an itemized invoice for your travel insurance.

  • Licensed Mexican physicians with verifiable cédulas profesionales
  • Downtown walk-in clinic plus house calls to any hotel, villa or yacht
  • Video visits when you would rather not leave your room
  • Transparent USD pricing, card or cash, and English invoices
