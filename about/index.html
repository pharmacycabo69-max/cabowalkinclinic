About Cabo Walk-In Clinic

Cabo Walk-In Clinic helps visitors and residents of Cabo San Lucas and Los Cabos see English-speaking doctors and nurses without long waits: in our downtown clinic, at your hotel or villa, or by video.

What we offer

  • Walk-in doctor visits from $80 USD
  • Home and hotel doctor visits from $200 USD, 24/7
  • IV hydration and recovery drips from $149 USD
  • Video consultations at $59 USD
  • Nurse care and lab tests

Where we serve

From downtown Cabo San Lucas to the Tourist Corridor and San José del Cabo, our team comes to your hotel or villa, or you can visit us downtown.
