Frequently asked questions

Where is Cabo Walk-In Clinic?

On Francisco I. Madero S/N, between Vicente Guerrero and Miguel Hidalgo, in downtown Cabo San Lucas (Plus Code V3MP+4M).

What are the clinic hours?

Daily 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Visits from 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM are by appointment and subject to doctor confirmation.

Can I pick up a prescription at a pharmacy?

Yes, at an RMC Pharmacy store near the Marina, with a valid prescription when the medicine requires one.
