Booking questions

What if I need to cancel?

Message us as soon as possible. If our team has not yet left for your location, there is no charge. See our refund policy for details.

Do I have to pay online?

No. Clinic visits can be paid at the clinic, and you can also walk in 9 AM–5 PM without booking. For house calls and IV therapy you can hold your time with a 50% card deposit, or send a request and pay once we confirm. If you paid a deposit, the balance is paid after the service.

Is my request confirmed when I send it?

Not yet. You receive a folio and the status “received, pending confirmation”. It is confirmed when our team contacts you with the time. We receive online requests 24/7; visits outside clinic hours depend on doctor availability.

Can I book for today?

Yes. Pick today’s date if a time is available, or message us on WhatsApp and we will fit you in as soon as possible.

Can I use my insurance?

We accept international health and travel insurance from the United States and Canada, subject to policy verification and coverage confirmation. See how verification works.

Do you come to my hotel?

Yes. Doctors and nurses visit any hotel, all-inclusive resort, villa, Airbnb or yacht in Los Cabos.
