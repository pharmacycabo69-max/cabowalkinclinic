Frequently asked questions

Can I get an X-ray today in Cabo San Lucas?

Usually yes. Message us on WhatsApp or walk in to our downtown clinic between 9 AM and 5 PM. For a portable X-ray at your hotel we confirm the time with you.

Do you do X-rays at my hotel?

Yes. Our radiology team brings a portable X-ray unit to your hotel, villa or Airbnb in Los Cabos.

Do I need a doctor’s order?

Yes. X-rays are taken with a medical order. Our doctor can see you first at the clinic, at your hotel or by video, or you can bring an order from your own doctor.

How much does an X-ray cost?

It depends on the area and the number of views, and whether it is at the clinic or at your hotel. Message us and we send a USD quote before anything is done.

Can you do CT, MRI or ultrasound?

We coordinate CT, MRI and ultrasound at a partner hospital in Los Cabos and our doctor explains the results to you in English.

Will my travel insurance pay?

Most travel-insurance policies cover medically necessary X-rays. You receive an itemized invoice in English to submit for reimbursement.

Need an X-ray in Cabo?

Tell us what happened and where you are staying. We reply with a quote and the next available time.
