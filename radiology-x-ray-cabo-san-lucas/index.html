Frequently asked questions

Do you have an X-ray room at the clinic?

No. We coordinate X-rays with our licensed radiology collaborators and confirm where the study is taken when we send your quote.

Do I need a doctor’s order?

Yes. Our doctor can see you at the clinic, at your hotel or by video, or you can bring an order from your own doctor.

How much is an X-ray?

It depends on the area, the number of views and the location. You get a USD quote before anything is done.

Can the X-ray be taken at my hotel?

Only on request and after confirmation of equipment, location and medical order. Most studies are done at the collaborator’s site.

Can I use my travel insurance?

US and Canada insurance is accepted subject to verification; you receive an itemized invoice.

Think you need an X-ray?

Send us what happened; a doctor tells you the next step and the quote.
