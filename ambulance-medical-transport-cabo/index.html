Travel Insurance & Medical Repatriation — What Most Travelers Don't Know

International air ambulance and medical repatriation typically run between US $25,000 and US $60,000 for a Mexico-to-USA or Mexico-to-Canada flight, and the U.S. Centers for Disease Control explicitly recommends international travelers carry medical-evacuation coverage. The good news: most quality travel-insurance policies — Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global, World Nomads, Manulife, Blue Cross Canada, and many others — include emergency medical evacuation and repatriation benefits, often with limits of $250,000 to $1,000,000. The catch: medevac claims require pre-approval from your insurer’s emergency assistance line, with the right medical documentation, in writing, fast.

That is exactly what we coordinate for you, in English, while you focus on getting better. We help you call your insurer, gather the medical reports they need, and arrange the receiving hospital so they can authorize the flight. We promise the process, not the result — every insurer has its own rules — but we make every step easier and faster than going it alone.

Pre-Trip Action Item

Verify your travel-insurance policy includes emergency medical evacuation and repatriation with at least $250,000 of coverage for Mexico.

It is the single most important line item for any traveler to Los Cabos. Without it, an air-ambulance flight home can cost out of pocket what a year's worth of premium would have covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Ambulance & Repatriation in Cabo

Do you provide air ambulance from Los Cabos to the USA and Canada?

Yes — we coordinate international air ambulance and medical repatriation flights from Los Cabos to hospitals across the United States and Canada, with a medical escort on board. We handle the flight logistics, ground transport at both ends, the receiving-hospital hand-off, and the insurance paperwork in English.

How much does medical repatriation from Mexico to the USA or Canada cost?

Air ambulance flights from Mexico to the USA or Canada typically range from US $25,000 to US $60,000 depending on destination, medical complexity and aircraft type. Many travel-insurance policies include medical evacuation and repatriation coverage of $250,000 to $1,000,000 — pre-approval is required, and we coordinate that for you.

How fast can a ground ambulance reach my hotel in Cabo?

Response times depend on traffic and your exact location, but our ground ambulances are positioned to reach most hotels and villas in Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo quickly. For an immediate life-threatening emergency, always call local emergency services (911 in Mexico) first; in parallel, call our 24/7 line and we will coordinate everything.

Will my travel insurance pay for the ambulance and air ambulance?

Most quality travel-insurance policies (Allianz, GeoBlue, IMG, Aetna International, Cigna Global, World Nomads, Manulife, Blue Cross and others) include emergency medical evacuation and repatriation benefits. Coverage and limits vary by policy. We help you contact your insurer’s emergency assistance line, gather the medical documentation they need, and request pre-approval. We provide a fully itemized invoice in English with medical codes. We cannot guarantee what your insurer will reimburse, but we make every step easier.

What if I need an ambulance from the San José del Cabo airport (SJD)?

We dispatch ground ambulance to and from Los Cabos International Airport (SJD) — for travelers who become ill mid-flight, for patients flying out commercially with a medical escort, and for transfer to an air ambulance on the tarmac. Call our 24/7 line.

Do you only transport to your partner hospital?

No. Medical decisions are made on clinical grounds. We transport you to the most appropriate hospital for your condition. For major emergencies, our partner hospital is a leading private hospital in Los Cabos with internationally certified specialists, ICU and surgery — but the patient and the family always decide.
