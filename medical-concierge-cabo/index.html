Frequently asked questions

Is this a membership or do I pay per visit?

There is no required membership. You pay per service — quoted up front in USD. Standard pricing: clinic visit from $80 USD, video visit $59 USD, hotel or villa house call $200 USD, IV therapy from $149 USD. Lab tests, nurse visits, hospital coordination and ambulance are quoted by service. For travel groups, wedding parties and corporate retreats we offer custom packages.

Can you handle our wedding, retreat or family trip?

Yes. We have served wedding parties, corporate retreats, bachelorette weekends and multi-generation family trips. We can do a pre-trip medical briefing for the planner, verify travel insurance for your guests, and have a physician on call dedicated to your party for the dates of the event. Contact us at least two weeks before for full coordination.

Do you take insurance?

At our clinic and for house calls we don’t bill insurance directly — most travelers pay at time of service and submit our itemized English invoice for reimbursement, which is faster and avoids surprises. For hospital-level care our partner hospital works with a wide network of national and international insurers, and we help you verify yours before any major step.

How fast do you respond?

Most house calls in Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo and the Tourist Corridor arrive within 45 minutes. Video visits are usually same-hour. We are bilingual on the phone 24/7.

What if I need a specialist or hospital admission?

We coordinate with a leading private hospital in Los Cabos that has internationally certified specialists, ICU, operating rooms and advanced imaging — all bilingual. We transport you with our ambulance, hand you off in English, and stay with you as your advocate. If your situation needs care closer to home, we coordinate ambulance and air ambulance repatriation to the USA or Canada.

Can you help me find an English-speaking doctor?

Yes. Our doctors and nurses speak English, and our team can coordinate your visit by phone or WhatsApp.

Important: Cabo Walk-In Clinic is an English-speaking clinic. Hospital and specialist care is delivered by an independent licensed hospital and its physicians; its pricing and insurance arrangements are set by that hospital. We coordinate the process but cannot guarantee reimbursement outcomes. For an immediate life-threatening emergency call 911 in Mexico first.
