Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

How much is a house call?

House calls start at $200 USD. We confirm the exact price by message before the doctor leaves.

How fast can a doctor arrive?

Usually 30–45 minutes in Cabo San Lucas and Pedregal, and 45–75 minutes on the Corridor and in San José del Cabo, depending on traffic.

Is it available at night?

Yes. Our mobile service runs 24/7. The walk-in clinic is open 9 AM to 10 PM daily.

Do you accept travel insurance?

We do not bill insurers directly, but we give you an itemized invoice in English for reimbursement.

Is this for emergencies?

No. For emergencies call 911.
