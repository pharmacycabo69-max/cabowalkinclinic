Frequently asked questions

Everything U.S. and Canadian families ask us first

How do you do background checks on caregivers in Mexico?

Every caregiver passes INE identity verification, CURP federal-registry validation, a state-issued Carta de No Antecedentes Penales (criminal-record clearance), two-plus reference checks, and current CPR certification. Dementia caregivers add Alzheimer’s Association essentiALZ training on top.

What happens if there’s a medical emergency?

Our 24/7 dispatch line connects to an on-call physician through our sister clinic, Cabo Walk-In Clinic. We can send a doctor to the home, coordinate ambulance transport to CIMA Hospital, Saint Luke’s, San José Hospital, or H+ Los Cabos — and we stay on the phone with the family abroad the entire time.

Do you serve snowbirds and seasonal residents?

Yes. Our Snowbird Plans activate when you arrive in Cabo and pause when you fly back. Annual home check-in visits while you’re abroad are included in higher-tier retainers, so your Cabo home is monitored even when no one’s there.

We only need someone for two weeks after surgery — do you do that?

Absolutely. Post-hospital recovery is one of our most-requested service lines. We’ll meet the discharge team at the hospital, transport home, and run 1-, 2-, or 4-week recovery packages with daily check-ins and wound monitoring.

My mother doesn’t want a “caregiver” — she’s very independent.

We hear this often. We start as a companion — someone who joins her for coffee, drives her to the art walk, plays cards, and respects her independence. Care escalates only as she invites it. Many of our most engaged clients started this way.

How do payments work? Can my family in the U.S. pay?

Yes. Billing is in USD by credit card, with itemized receipts emailed to whoever you choose — the senior, the spouse, an adult child abroad, or all three. Mexican CFDI invoices available on request.

Does Medicare or U.S. health insurance cover this?

Medicare does not pay for healthcare outside the U.S. Some international expat insurance plans (Allianz Worldwide Care, Cigna Global, BlueCross International, GeoBlue, IMG) reimburse in-home care — we provide the documentation you need to submit a claim.
