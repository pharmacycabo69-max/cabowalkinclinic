# Domain strategy — Cabo Walk-In Clinic (V4 §9)

- Intent owned: **physical clinic + diagnosis** in downtown Cabo San Lucas (walk-in doctor, English-speaking doctor, urgent care non-emergency, X-ray, lab), plus house calls and video visits. IV is complementary.
- Not owned here: pharmacy, delivery, store locations (owner instruction), aesthetic Ads traffic (organic only, footer/About links).
- Overlap with PharmacyDoctors (IV, mobile care): keep IV pages but lead with clinic intent; do not duplicate PharmacyDoctors copy.
- Spanish: 6 core pages for residents and Spanish-speaking visitors; extend only with original content.
- Hotel-specific pages: legacy ones redirect; do not recreate near-identical hotel pages or imply resort affiliation.
- Hypotheses to validate with GSC/Ads data: walk-in vs house-call share of conversions; ES page impressions after 30/60/90 days.
