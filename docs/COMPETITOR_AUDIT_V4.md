## 3. International best-in-class (design + conversion)
Read: One Medical, ZoomCare, CityMD, Parsley Health, SkinSpirit. Not readable: Carbon Health (403), GoHealth (403), The DRIPBAR (JS only), Reviver (DNS), hoteldoctor.co.uk (parked).

Patterns adopted (ranked):
1. Reason-first hero with three clear paths (walk-in / video / house call) — ZoomCare, Parsley.
2. Self-pay prices with what is and isn't included — ZoomCare.
3. Symptom-language entry cards ("I'm sick", "I'm injured", "I need a test") deep-linking to booking — CityMD, ZoomCare.
4. Numbered booking steps that disclose what is asked first — Parsley /labs.
5. Clinician line with degree, licence and languages — Parsley.
6. Specific, reason-tagged real reviews — ZoomCare.
7. Tap-to-call/WhatsApp visible in header and after key sections — ZoomCare.
8. "Clinic vs ER vs house call" comparison to help choose — Parsley comparison table.
9. Location block with address, hours and directions next to the CTA — CityMD/SkinSpirit.
10. Payment methods + currency near prices — Parsley, SkinSpirit.

Avoided (generic / AI-template signals observed even on big brands): lorem/placeholder blocks, duplicated sections, conflicting numbers across pages, abstract slogan heroes, six-card vague-benefit icon grids, dead "Learn more" links, stock heroes unrelated to the clinic, auto-rotating carousels and email popups over the first screen.
