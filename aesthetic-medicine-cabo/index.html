Frequently asked questions

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every aesthetic treatment starts with a medical evaluation at our clinic in downtown Cabo San Lucas. The doctor confirms whether a treatment is right for you before anything is done.

Who performs the evaluation?

A licensed physician of Cabo Walk-In Clinic. Dra. Rosibel Núñez Rojo, Médico Cirujano (cédula 7408277), leads our aesthetic medicine area.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the treatment and the amount you need, so the doctor gives you a clear quote after your evaluation, before you decide.

Can I have a treatment while on vacation in Cabo?

Often yes. Book early in your stay and tell us your dates, so the doctor can plan the treatment and any follow-up around your trip, including sun and pool advice.

Will it look natural?

Our approach is to keep you looking like yourself. The doctor plans the treatment around your features and tells you honestly if a request would not suit you.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results vary from person to person. The doctor explains realistic expectations, possible risks and aftercare before you decide.
