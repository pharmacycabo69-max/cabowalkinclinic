Aesthetic medicine in Cabo San Lucas: what to expect

Cabo Walk-In Clinic offers aesthetic medicine in downtown Cabo San Lucas for visitors and residents who want a fresher, rested look without an overdone result. Treatments include lip enhancement, facial botulinum toxin for expression lines, skin boosters and collagen stimulators for facial rejuvenation, and depigmenting peels for dark spots.

Your first visit is a free medical evaluation with a licensed physician, who reviews your health history, medications and goals, examines the area and explains your options, expected results, risks, aftercare and the final price in USD before you decide. If a treatment is not right for you, we tell you.

Many patients book during their stay in Los Cabos. Ask us about timing around beach days, sun exposure and your travel dates, and keep our WhatsApp for any question after your treatment. Frequently asked questions

How much do lip fillers cost in Cabo San Lucas?

Lip enhancement with hyaluronic acid is $600 USD plus tax. A 3-syringe package to combine areas such as lips, chin, jawline or nose is $1,600 USD plus tax. The final price is confirmed at your evaluation.

How much is a botulinum toxin treatment for forehead lines?

The upper face (frown lines, forehead and crow’s feet) is $550 USD plus tax. Single areas such as a brow tail lift, gummy smile or jawline start at $180 USD plus tax.

Is the evaluation free?

Yes. Every aesthetic medicine evaluation at our downtown Cabo San Lucas clinic is free, whichever treatment you are considering. You only pay if you decide to go ahead with a treatment.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every aesthetic treatment starts with a medical evaluation at our clinic in downtown Cabo San Lucas. The doctor confirms whether a treatment is right for you before anything is done.

Who performs the evaluation?

A licensed physician of Cabo Walk-In Clinic. Dra. Rosibel Núñez Rojo, Médico Cirujano (cédula 7408277), leads our aesthetic medicine area.

Can I have a treatment while on vacation in Cabo?

Often yes. Book early in your stay and tell us your dates, so the doctor can plan the treatment and any follow-up around your trip, including sun and pool advice.

Will it look natural?

Our approach is to keep you looking like yourself. The doctor plans the treatment around your features and tells you honestly if a request would not suit you.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results vary from person to person. The doctor explains realistic expectations, possible risks and aftercare before you decide.
