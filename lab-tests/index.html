Frequently asked questions

Can a nurse draw blood at my hotel in Cabo?

Yes. A nurse can come to your hotel, villa or Airbnb in Los Cabos, or you can visit our downtown clinic.

How much do lab tests cost?

It depends on the tests. Message us and we send a USD quote before anything is done.

How long do results take?

It depends on the study. We tell you the expected time with your quote.

Do I need to fast?

Some tests, like glucose or lipids, may require fasting. We tell you how to prepare when we confirm your appointment.

Can a doctor explain my results?

Yes. An English-speaking doctor can review them with you at the clinic, at your hotel or by video.
