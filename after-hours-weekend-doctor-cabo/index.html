When you should still go to the ER

After-hours convenience doesn’t change the rules for emergencies. Chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke signs, severe injury or bleeding, or a high fever with a stiff neck mean a hospital or 911, day or night — and we’ll tell you so honestly and help coordinate transport. For everything else — the midnight fever, the Sunday stomach bug, the holiday-weekend ear infection — an after-hours house call is faster, calmer and cheaper than the ER.

What we handle after hours

High fevers, stomach bugs and food poisoning, infections, allergic reactions, migraines, dehydration, minor injuries and prescription needs. A bilingual doctor at your hotel keeps the night manageable.

Peace of mind through the night

The best time to save our number is before you need it. When illness strikes at 2am, you don’t want to be searching an unfamiliar phone while caring for a sick child or partner. Keep our contact saved, and when you call after hours, have a few things ready: who the patient is and their age, the main symptoms and when they started, any existing conditions or regular medications, and your exact hotel and room. That lets us triage instantly and dispatch the right doctor with an accurate arrival window. While you wait, basic steps help: keep the patient hydrated with small sips if they can tolerate it, use a light layer and a cool cloth for fever, and don’t give medication you’re unsure about — the doctor will advise. Most night and weekend calls are routine problems that simply happened at an inconvenient hour, and they’re exactly what we’re set up to handle. Knowing a bilingual physician can be at your door within the hour, any hour, is often the difference between a stressful night and a manageable one.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a doctor in Cabo at night or on a Sunday?

Yes. Our bilingual doctor service operates 24/7, including nights, Sundays and holidays. A physician comes to your hotel for a flat $200 USD when clinics and pharmacies are closed.

Are pharmacies open late in Cabo?

Many close at night or on Sundays and holidays. A doctor can treat you on the spot at your hotel and tell you where to fill any prescription.

Should I use a house call or the ER after hours?

For non-emergencies — fevers, stomach bugs, infections, minor injuries — an after-hours house call is faster and cheaper. For chest pain, breathing trouble, stroke signs or severe injury, go to a hospital or call 911.
