What urgent care covers (and what it doesn’t)

Urgent care is for problems that need same-day attention but aren’t life-threatening: high fevers, severe stomach upset and dehydration, infections (ear, sinus, urinary, skin), allergic reactions, migraines, sprains and minor injuries, cuts needing care, and sudden flare-ups of a known condition. What it doesn’t cover is true emergencies — chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs, major injury — which need a hospital or 911. If you’re unsure, a quick video visit can triage you.

Why urgent care beats the ER for non-emergencies

A foreign emergency room means long waits, a possible large up-front deposit, and a language barrier when you’re already stressed. Urgent care with us is the opposite: seen quickly, a clear flat price known in advance, everything in English, and treatment on the spot with prescriptions and an itemized invoice for insurance. We keep the ER for what it’s truly for — and help coordinate transport if you need it.

How quickly can urgent care reach you

Speed is the whole point of urgent care, so here’s the honest picture. If you can travel, our downtown clinic sees walk-ins from 9am to 10pm with no appointment — you’re typically seen quickly rather than waiting hours as you might at a hospital. If you’d rather not move, a doctor comes to your hotel 24/7, usually within about an hour depending on your location and current demand; we always give you a live arrival window when you call. And if you’re not sure whether your problem even needs an in-person visit, a video consultation with a bilingual doctor can triage you — they’ll tell you to rest and rehydrate, recommend treatment, or arrange to be seen. That tiered approach means you get the right level of care at the right speed without defaulting to an expensive, slow emergency room for something minor. Save our number now so that when something flares up, help is one message away rather than a stressful search on an unfamiliar phone in an unfamiliar town.
