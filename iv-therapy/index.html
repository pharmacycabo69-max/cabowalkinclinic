How it works

Three simple steps. Self-pay, or verified US/Canada insurance.

1

Pick a drip

Message us the drip you want, or ask which one fits your day. We confirm price and time.

2

A nurse comes to you

An English-speaking nurse arrives at your hotel, villa or yacht, or you visit our downtown clinic.

3

Relax during your drip

The session usually takes 45–60 minutes while you rest. Pay by card or cash.

Deposit: 50% is paid by card with Stripe when you book; the balance is paid after the service (refund policy). A licensed professional performs a brief health screening before every service. Not for emergencies — call 911.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

How much is an IV drip?

Drips start at $159 USD, tax included. The exact price depends on the blend and is confirmed before you book.

How long does it take?

We confirm the nurse’s arrival time with you when you book. The drip itself takes about 45–60 minutes.

Is there a health screening?

Yes. A brief health screening is done before every service.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Groups of 6 or more pay $119 per person, tax included.

Is this for emergencies?

No. For emergencies call 911.
